## Overview

This site is operated by Samuel Matildes. It publishes technical documentation and may use Google AdSense to display advertising. It does not require an account to read its content.

## Advertising and consent

Google AdSense may use cookies, device identifiers, IP address, and related technical information to deliver and measure advertisements. Where required, the site presents Google's consent message before advertising that relies on consent is used. You can use that message to accept, refuse, or manage your choices.

Google and its partners may process data according to their own policies. For more information, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider and network infrastructure may process standard technical data, such as IP address, browser details, request time, and requested URLs, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. This site does not intentionally collect personal data through reader accounts, comments, or contact forms.

## Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings and through the advertising consent choices shown on the site where available. Blocking cookies may affect the relevance or availability of advertising, but should not prevent access to the documentation.
